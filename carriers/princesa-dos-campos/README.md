# Princesa dos Campos

Princesa dos Campos parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Princesa dos Campos is an intercity bus company from Ponta Grossa, Paraná. Its parcel service carries
shipments on its routes across Paraná and the neighbouring states.

## Numbers

Its tracker takes the sender's or recipient's CPF or CNPJ with an invoice (nota fiscal) number. It
has no field for a parcel number of its own, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Every lookup needs a CPF or CNPJ.

## Links

Lookups need the tax id, so there are no tracking links.
