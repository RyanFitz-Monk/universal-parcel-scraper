# Airtrans

Airtrans parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Airtrans Group Ltd of Nicosia is a forwarder, ground handler and courier in Cyprus, with cargo
handling at Larnaca Airport. Its domestic courier delivers to doors and pickup points and handles
online shops' last-mile deliveries; its international courier works through partner networks, whose
items can carry the partner's numbers. It holds a general postal authorisation from OCECPR.

## Numbers

No public page shows a whole number or its layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

The header form on `www.airtransgroup.com` sends the number as `param` to `/results/`, which renders
the result on the server. With no public number, what it shows for a shipment is unknown.

## Links

`www.airtransgroup.com/results/?param=<number>` opens one shipment.
