# easyCourier

easyCourier parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

easyCourier is the brand of Svelta Courier (Cyprus) Ltd of Limassol, a courier with its own drivers,
same-day delivery across Cyprus, a line from Greece, parcel lockers (easyLockers) and partner service
points. It holds a general postal authorisation from OCECPR.

## Numbers

No public page shows a whole number or its layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

The home page's form opens `/Home/LiveTracking` with the number as `TrackingId`; the page shows the
shipment's live tracking and time estimate. With no public number to check its replies against,
there is no adapter.

## Links

`www.easycourier.com.cy/Home/LiveTracking?TrackingId=<number>` opens one shipment.
