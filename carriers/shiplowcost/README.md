# ShipLowCost

ShipLowCost parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

ShipLowCost is the parcel service of Express Trailers Ltd of Luqa, part of Malta's Express Group. It
consolidates parcels to Malta from hubs in the UK and other European countries and sends parcels from
Malta abroad. It is on the Malta Communications Authority's register of postal service providers.

## Numbers

No public page shows a whole number or its layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

The home page's tracking widget takes the number through an ASP.NET form postback. With no public
number to check its replies against, there is no adapter.

## Links

The tracker takes numbers only through its form, so no address opens one shipment.
