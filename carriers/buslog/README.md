# Buslog

Buslog parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Buslog carries parcels and part loads in the luggage holds of Grupo JCA's bus lines, among them 1001,
Cometa and Catarinense, between more than 170 cities in São Paulo, Rio de Janeiro, Minas Gerais and
the South, with stores of its own.

## Numbers

Its tracker takes the parcel (encomenda) number, an invoice number with the shipper's CNPJ, or the
recipient's CPF or CNPJ with a postcode. No public page shows a Buslog number, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

The form opens the lookup on Track3r, a tracking platform other carriers use too, whose page carries
a Cloudflare Turnstile check. No public number is known to check its replies against.

## Links

Buslog's page builds the Track3r address with a key it gives every visitor. The catalog sends people
to Buslog's own tracking page instead.
