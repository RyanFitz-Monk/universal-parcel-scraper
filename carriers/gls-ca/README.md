# GLS Canada

GLS Canada parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

GLS Canada, formerly Dicom Transportation Group, carries parcels and freight across Canada from
its base in Montreal. It belongs to the GLS group but runs a system of its own: its numbers are
not those of GLS's European networks, so bare "GLS" stays their name, with their clocks.

## Numbers

No public page shows a GLS Canada number or its layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

No public number is known to check the tracking page's replies against.

## Links

The tracking page reads numbers from its `match` parameter; the consignee's postal code is
optional.
