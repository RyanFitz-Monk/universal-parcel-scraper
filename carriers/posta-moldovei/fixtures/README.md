# Fixtures

Both are replies of `trimiteri-api.posta.md/public/track/{number}` for public sample items listed
in [`numbers.json`](../numbers.json), as the Internet Archive kept them; the names fields are
emptied.

- `inbound-failed-attempt.json`: an item from the Netherlands, its posting and dispatch in
  English, its arrival and departure at Chișinău's office of exchange, a failed delivery attempt
  dated by day and its arrival at a parcel terminal.
- `inbound-customs.json`: an item from Germany with the wordings in Romanian, customs at
  Chișinău's office of exchange and its arrival at the delivery office, named by its postcode.
