# Western Post

Western Post parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Western Post (西邮物流) of Shenzhen ships large items for Chinese online sellers to its own
warehouses in North America and Europe and delivers them over the last mile with its own trucks
and partners.

## Numbers

The tracking box on its home page takes one or more numbers separated by spaces. No public page
shows one, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

No public number is known to check its tracker's replies against.

## Leads

The tracking box posts the numbers, joined by commas, as `trackNumberList` to
`https://www.wpglb.com/trackQuery`. The English pages have no tracking box.

## Links

The tracking box answers only by POST, so no address opens one shipment.
