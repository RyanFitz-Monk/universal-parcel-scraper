# Afimex

Afimex parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

AFIMEX Paquetería y Logística is a parcel carrier in Monterrey, Nuevo León. It collects and delivers
across Mexico, tries a home delivery up to three times, and gives business customers an API.

## Numbers

The site's help says the guide is the eight digits after the label's second hyphen. No public page
shows a whole guide, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

The tracker posts up to 25 guides, one per line, with a CSRF token to `/rastrea-envio-resultado-v2`.
No public guide is known to check its replies against.

## Links

The tracker takes guides only in its form, so the catalog sends people to the tracking page.
