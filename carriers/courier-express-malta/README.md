# Courier Express Malta

Courier Express Malta parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Courier Express Malta Ltd of Sliema stores, packs and delivers online shops' orders across Malta and
Gozo, with cash on delivery and photo proof of delivery. It is on the Malta Communications
Authority's register of postal service providers as Courier Express Ltd.

## Numbers

No public page shows a whole number or its layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracker, `track.courierexpressmalta.com`, is a white label of the Onro delivery platform. It
reads the number from its address and shows the courier's position and time estimate, the stops,
the delivery status and the proof of delivery. With no public number to check its replies against,
there is no adapter.

## Links

`track.courierexpressmalta.com/<number>` opens one delivery; the tracker also takes `/d/<number>`.
