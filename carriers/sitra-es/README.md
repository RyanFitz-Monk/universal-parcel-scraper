# SITRA

SITRA parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

SITRA is a courier in Alcorcón, Madrid, for business documents and parcels and same-day direct
deliveries in the Comunidad de Madrid. Parcels it sends on through national and international
networks are tracked at those networks; its own deliveries are on its IRIS tracker, whose tenant
is named after the town.

## Numbers

No public page shows a SITRA number, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

The tracker is the company's tenant of the IRIS courier software on `alcorcon.factapp.es`. It
posts an ASP.NET form, so a number cannot go in the address. No public number is known to check
its replies against.

## Links

The tracker takes numbers only in its form, so the catalog sends people to the tracking page.
