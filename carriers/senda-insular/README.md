# Senda Insular

Senda Insular parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Senda Insular, of Barcelona, carries urgent shipments from mainland Spain to the Balearic and
Canary Islands, Ceuta and Melilla, and clears customs for them. Its tracker asks for the
destination postcode beside the shipment number.

## Numbers

No public page shows a Senda Insular number, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

The site's form sends the number as `seg` and the postcode as `cp` to its Direcline tracker. No
public number is known to check its replies against.

## Links

The tracker needs the postcode beside the number, so the catalog sends people to the tracking
page.
