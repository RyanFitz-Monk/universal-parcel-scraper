# Rápido Ochoa

Rápido Ochoa parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Rápido Ochoa is an intercity bus company from Medellín whose parcel unit carries parcels, freight
and courier shipments through more than fifty agencies.

## Numbers

Its tracker, on the TM Solutions freight platform, takes a guide number or a document number. No
public page shows a Rápido Ochoa number, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

No public number is known to check its tracker's replies against.

## Links

The tracker is a form that posts back to its own page, so there are no tracking links.
