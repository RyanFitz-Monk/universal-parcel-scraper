# Extrans Global

Extrans Global parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

EXTRANS International (HK), 旭晖空运国际, is a Korean-owned Hong Kong forwarder founded in 1999, with
mainland branches under 广州瑛特仕国际货运代理有限公司. Its e-commerce division ships parcels from
Hong Kong, Korea and Vietnam hubs, and its site extransglobal.com sends customers to its order
system on the ITDIDA platform, extrans.itdida.com.

## Numbers

No public page shows an Extrans e-commerce number, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

No public number is known to check the portal's replies against. Its air and ocean bills are
tracked on another page.

## Leads

The portal's platform answers `GET extrans.itdida.com/itdida-api/queryTracks?no=` with JSON, as for
the other ITDIDA tenants in the catalog.

## Links

The portal's tracking page takes the number in its form, so no address opens one parcel.
