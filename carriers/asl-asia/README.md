# ASL

ASL parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

ASL (Advanced & Simple Logistics) of Hong Kong ships and fulfils orders for online sellers, with
offices in mainland China, Vietnam, the United States and the United Kingdom.

## Numbers

Its Track & Trace page takes up to twenty numbers, one per line. No public page shows one, so
there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

No public number is known to check its tracker's replies against.

## Leads

The Track & Trace page posts the numbers as JSON to
`portal.asl-asia.net/api/v1/tracking/track-routings`.

## Links

The page takes numbers only in its form, so no address opens one shipment.
