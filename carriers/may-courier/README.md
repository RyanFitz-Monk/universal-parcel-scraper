# May Courier

May Courier parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

May Courier is a Madrid courier for national and international deliveries and collections, air
cargo with customs clearance, and biological samples.

## Numbers

The tracker takes the delivery note (albarán) number, up to 20 characters. No public page shows
one, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

The tracking form on every page posts the number to `/consulta-tu-envio`. No public number is
known to check its replies against.

## Links

The tracker takes numbers only in its form, so the catalog sends people to the tracking page.
