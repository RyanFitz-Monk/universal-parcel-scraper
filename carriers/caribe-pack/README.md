# Caribe Pack

Caribe Pack parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Caribe Pack carries parcels and freight on the routes of Caribe Tours, a Dominican intercity bus
company, across the Dominican Republic and to Haiti.

## Numbers

No public page shows a Caribe Pack number, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

No public number is known to check its tracker's replies against.

## Links

Caribe Tours links its tracker, a page on the iPlus courier platform, without a number, so there are
no tracking links.
