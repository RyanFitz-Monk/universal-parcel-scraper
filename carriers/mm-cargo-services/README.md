# MM Cargo Services

MM Cargo Services parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

MM Cargo Services is a freight forwarder in Johannesburg with an office in Kitwe, Zambia. It moves
goods by road, sea and air across Southern Africa and clears them through customs.

## Numbers

Its tracker takes the code from its WhatsApp and email updates, MM- and two groups of four letters
and digits, or a parcel's tracking number. No public page shows an MM Cargo Services code, so there
is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

No public number is known to check its tracker's replies against.

## Links

`mmcargoservices.co.za/track?number=<number>` looks one number up, and the library reads the number
from that address.
