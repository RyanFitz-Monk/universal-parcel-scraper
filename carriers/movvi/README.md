# Movvi

Movvi parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Movvi, formerly Meridional, is a road carrier founded in 1988 with its head office in Campinas. It
carries part loads, express and e-commerce parcels, pharmaceuticals and air freight between branches
in the South and Southeast, and delivers to more than 3,000 cities.

## Numbers

Its tracker takes an invoice (nota fiscal) or CT-e number with the sender's or recipient's CPF or
CNPJ. It has no field for a Movvi number, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Every lookup needs a CPF or CNPJ.

## Links

Lookups need the tax id, so there are no tracking links.
