# Movicarga

Movicarga parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Movicarga Bajío, of León, Guanajuato, delivers parcels in Mexico and runs last-mile and middle-mile
transport and vehicle rental. Its site is movicargamx.mx.

## Numbers

Its tracker takes a reference number. No public page shows one, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

No public number is known to check its tracker's replies against.

## Links

The Rastrear mi paquete button opens its tracker on FarEye, a delivery management platform. The
tracker reads no number from its address, so there are no tracking links.
