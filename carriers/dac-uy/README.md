# DAC

DAC parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

DAC, the Agencia Central, is a Uruguayan parcel carrier with agencies and home delivery across the
country, based in Montevideo.

## Numbers

Its receipt shows a twelve-digit tracking number, and its tracker also takes the shop's order
number. No public page shows a real DAC number, and twelve digits are a shape many carriers share,
so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

The tracker posts the number with an invisible reCAPTCHA token. No public number is known to check
its tracker's replies against.

## Links

The tracking form posts its field, so there are no tracking links.
