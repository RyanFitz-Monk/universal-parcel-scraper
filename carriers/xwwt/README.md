# XWWT

XWWT parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

XWWT (兴伟万通, 深圳市兴伟万通供应链有限公司) is a Shenzhen cross-border operator of commercial
express and dedicated lines to the United States, Australia, Europe and Japan, with air and sea
freight. Its tracker is its page on a shared Chinese logistics system, `hnkh.rtb56.com`.

## Numbers

No public page shows an XWWT number, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

The tracker posts its ASP.NET form back to `track_query.aspx`. No public number is known to check
its replies against.

## Links

The tracking page reads numbers from its `track_number` parameter and searches on load.
