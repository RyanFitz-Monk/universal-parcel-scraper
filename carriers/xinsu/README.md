# Xinsu International

Xinsu International parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Xinsu International (新速国际, 广州新速国际供应链有限公司) is a Guangzhou cross-border forwarder
selling express and dedicated lines, FBA freight and consolidation. Its site is ixinsu.com, and its
tracker is its tenant on the Kingtrans forwarder platform.

## Numbers

No public page shows a Xinsu number, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

No public number is known to check the tracker's replies against.

## Links

The tracker's results page takes numbers from its `bills` parameter.
