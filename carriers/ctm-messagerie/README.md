# CTM Messagerie

CTM Messagerie parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

CTM Messagerie is the parcel service of CTM, Morocco's national coach company, with agencies across
the country.

## Numbers

Its tracker takes the number printed on the shipping declaration. No public page shows a CTM
Messagerie number, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

No public number is known to check its tracker's replies against.

## Links

The tracking form posts the number, so there are no tracking links.
