# JP Logistics

JP Logistics shipments use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

JP Logistics (JPロジスティクス) is the Japan Post group's business-to-business freight company:
less-than-truckload networks across Japan, contract logistics and international transport, from
head offices in Tokyo and Osaka. It was Toll Express Japan, and Footwork Express before that.

## Numbers

Its general cargo inquiry page (一般貨物照会) takes up to ten inquiry numbers (お問合せ番号) of
up to fifteen characters, typed without hyphens. No public page shows one, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

No public number is known to check its tracker's replies against.

## Links

The inquiry page answers only by POST, in Shift_JIS, so no address opens one shipment.
