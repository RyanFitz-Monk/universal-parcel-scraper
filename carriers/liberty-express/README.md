# Liberty Express

Liberty Express parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Liberty Express is a courier from Caracas that receives purchases at its warehouse in Doral,
Florida, and delivers them in Venezuela and other Latin American countries.

## Numbers

Its iQPack tracker takes a guide or tracking number. No public page shows a Liberty Express number,
so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

No public number is known to check its tracker's replies against.

## Links

The tracker posts the number with a form token, so there are no tracking links.
