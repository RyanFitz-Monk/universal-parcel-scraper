# Transnet Couriers

Transnet Couriers parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Transnet Couriers Ltd, of Leeds, runs same-day, overnight, European and worldwide express
courier services.

## Numbers

Its tracker takes an air waybill number. No public page shows one, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracker is its account on courier-bookings.com, courier software that gives each courier a
numbered subdomain. The tracking box is on the TRACKING page of its own site. No public number
is known to check its tracker's replies against.

## Links

`30584626.courier-bookings.com/trackit.aspx?AWB=<number>` opens one consignment, and the
library reads the number from `AWB`. Other couriers' subdomains have no rule.
