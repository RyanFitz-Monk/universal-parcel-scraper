# Veøy

Veøy parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Veøy is one of Norway's largest privately owned transport companies, based in Åndalsnes, with
offices across Norway and the group companies Veøy Møre, Tangvik and Veøy Buss. It carries goods by
road and rail, among them medicines, temperature-controlled goods, part loads and general cargo,
fish and long, wide and heavy loads, and offers warehousing.

## Numbers

No public page shows a Veøy number, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

The tracker is Veøy's page in SmartSys Track and Trace, an OutSystems app that takes the tenant as
`TenantGUID` and the number as `SearchFor`. Veøy Møre has its own tracker, served only over plain
HTTP. No public number is known to check its replies against.

## Links

The site's Sporing av gods button for Veøy opens the SmartSys page; the catalog adds the number as
`SearchFor`.
