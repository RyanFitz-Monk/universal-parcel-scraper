# USEA EXPRESS

USEA EXPRESS parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

USEA EXPRESS ships for cross-border e-commerce sellers from China to Europe by rail, truck, sea
and air, with its own customs clearance in several European countries, overseas warehouses and
deliveries to Amazon warehouses.

## Numbers

The number field on its tracking page shows two numbers made of `USEA` and fourteen digits as
examples. No other public page shows one, so no rule claims them.

## Why there is no adapter

No public number is known to check its tracker's replies against.

## Leads

The tracking page is part of USEA's client ordering app at nxclient.usea56.com, built on
南讯数字科技 (Nanxun)'s logistics platform; its script reads tracking from
`/api/express/track/` paths on the same host.

## Links

The tracking page takes numbers only in its form, so no address opens one shipment.
