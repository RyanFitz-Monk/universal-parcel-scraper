# Neway Transport

Neway Transport shipments use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Neway Transport (Transport Aus Pty Ltd) has carried oversized, bulky, heavy and fragile goods on
Australia's East Coast since 1983, between Queensland, New South Wales and Victoria, with
two-person crews.

## Numbers

Its tracking page embeds WooDelivery's tracking form, which takes up to ten tracking IDs
separated by commas. No public page shows one, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

No public number is known to check its tracker's replies against.

## Leads

WooDelivery is a delivery-management platform many carriers share. The embedded form,
`app.woodelivery.com/Form/Track?Id=68EB9A03-9`, names Neway by its form id.

## Links

The form takes numbers only in its field, so no address opens one shipment.
