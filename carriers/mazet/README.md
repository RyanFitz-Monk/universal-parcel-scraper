# Mazet

Mazet parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Groupe Mazet, of Montélimar, is a family transport group for parcels, groupage, part and full
loads in France and Europe.

## Numbers

Its tracker takes an order reference with the delivery postcode and country. No public page
shows one, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Every lookup needs the delivery postcode and country, and the tracker posts them as JSON. No
public number is known to check its tracker's replies against.
