# Quick Couriers Service

Quick Couriers Service parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Quick Couriers Service is a Pakistani courier for online shops: same-city and nationwide
delivery, cash on delivery and returns. Its site runs on a shared courier platform. It is not
Quick Courier Service of the United States.

## Numbers

No public page shows a Quick Couriers Service number, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

No public number is known to check the tracker's replies against.

## Links

The tracking page reads numbers from its `CN` parameter.
