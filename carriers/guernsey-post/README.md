# Guernsey Post

Guernsey Post parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Guernsey Post is the national post of the Bailiwick of Guernsey, with Alderney and Sark.

## Numbers

It gives international items UPU S10 numbers ending in `GG`, which generic postal detection
handles, and its local Isle Deliver items numbers starting with `GPT`. Its tracker sends Royal
Mail, Parcelforce, Evri, FedEx and Yodel numbers to those carriers' pages. No public page shows
a Guernsey Post number, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

No public number is known to check its tracker's replies against.
