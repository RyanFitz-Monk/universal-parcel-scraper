# Fixtures

Both are replies of `rastreae.api.mandae.com.br/v2/rastreamentos/{code}` for public sample codes
listed in [`numbers.json`](../numbers.json), as Rastreaê's result page received them.

- `delivered-jt-hub.json`: a parcel posted via the partner `JT_HUB` under the partner's own code,
  moved between its units, out for delivery and delivered.
- `delivered-total-express.json`: an older parcel delivered by Total Express, received at the
  partner's unit before it was processed there.
