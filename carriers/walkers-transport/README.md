# Walkers Transport

Walkers Transport parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Walkers Transport, run by George Walkers Transport Limited of Leeds, distributes pallets across
the UK and Ireland and runs storage and freight to Ireland.

## Numbers

Its tracker takes a job number with its piece, or a customer reference, with the delivery
postcode. No public page shows one, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Every lookup needs the delivery postcode. No public number is known to check its tracker's
replies against.
