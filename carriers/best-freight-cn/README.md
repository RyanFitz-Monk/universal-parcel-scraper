# BEST Freight China

Tracks BEST's mainland China freight network through its official guest tracker.
BEST Express in other countries and the former Chinese express network are separate services.

`direct` requests `api/general/imageverifycode`, then posts the whole waybill in the
homepage's `billcode/quickly/list` request. A local CPU OCR worker requires agreement
between the original image and two median filters before answering. Each lookup owns
its cookies and challenge, with at most two fresh puzzles inside its deadline. The optional
`sharp` and `onnxruntime-web` peers are needed for OCR; providers remain available if it cannot run.

The reply must identify one whole waybill. Main and child-consignment scans retain their
local clocks and wording. Unknown scan types remain unresolved; child activity alone does
not establish whole-consignment delivery. Empty successful results use the official view's
not-found contract. Address, count and attachment fields are omitted.

The supported numeric shape overlaps other carriers and supplies no automatic recognition.
The homepage renders results in place and has no shipment-specific link.

`BEST_FREIGHT_CN_TRACKING_NUMBER=<public sample> npm run test:carriers:live -- carriers/best-freight-cn`
